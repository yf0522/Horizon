China&\#x27;s State Administration for Market Regulation and Standardization Administration have released the &quot;Automotive Software Quality and Defect Management Specification&quot; national standard. This new standard mandates comprehensive quality assurance across the entire software lifecycle for intelligent connected vehicles, covering requirements analysis, design, implementation, integration, and verification. The standard aims to shift quality control from &quot;post-incident handling&quot; to &quot;defect prevention&quot; and specifically regulates Over-The-Air \(OTA\) recalls for software defects, ensuring closed-loop defect resolution.
