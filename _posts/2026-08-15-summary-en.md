### [BDH-CQ: New AI Reasoning System Breaks Cost-Accuracy Frontier on ARC-AGI-1](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vov5r5/bdhcq_incontext_learning_with_recurrent_latent/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

BDH-CQ is a novel reasoning system that integrates in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, enabling it to update recurrent memory with task demonstrations and solve queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent workspace without verbalizing intermediate steps. This system unifies memory, adaptation, and inference within the same computational fabric, operating without parameter updates at inference time or the use of task identifiers in training. A 150M-parameter configuration achieved 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at a cost of $0.00070 per task, thereby breaking through the previously reported cost-accuracy Pareto frontier.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 15, 06:18

**「Background」** In-context learning allows AI models to adapt to new tasks by processing examples provided within the input, without updating their parameters. Recurrent latent reasoning involves iterative computation in a high-dimensional, non-linguistic space to solve problems. ARC-AGI-1 is a challenging benchmark introduced in 2019 to assess systematic generalization and compositional reasoning, which remained largely unbeaten for years despite significant advancements in large language models.

**「Impact」** BDH-CQ establishes a new state-of-the-art in cost efficiency for AI reasoning on the ARC-AGI-1 benchmark, achieving 29.5% pass@2 at $0.00070 per task and breaking the previous cost-accuracy Pareto frontier. This advancement provides AI researchers and developers with a more efficient benchmark for in-context learning and latent reasoning systems.

### [Anthropic Shares 6 Claude Code Cost-Saving Tips, Highlighting 90% Prompt Cache Savings](http://claude.md/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Anthropic has released six practical tips for optimizing the cost of using Claude Code, emphasizing that prompt caching can reduce expenses by up to 90%. Key strategies include using \`/clear\` after tasks to avoid sending irrelevant context, locking the model and inference strength to prevent prompt cache invalidation, and attaching files directly with \`@\` instead of typing paths. Additionally, developers should use silent parameters or sub-agents for verbose commands, run \`/compact\` before breaks to compress conversations while the cache is active, and utilize \`/context\` at the start of new sessions to manage loaded content. The company notes that output tokens are five times more expensive than input, while a prompt cache hit costs only 0.1 times the normal input price, contributing to significant savings for developers who currently spend an average of $13 daily on tokens.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 15, 11:14

**「Background」** Claude Code is a Visual Studio Code extension developed by Anthropic that integrates their Claude AI directly into the IDE, functioning as an agentic coding tool to assist developers with tasks like editing files and running commands. As with many AI tools, its operation involves processing tokens for both input and output, which incurs costs based on usage.

**「Impact」** These cost-saving strategies offer a direct and substantial financial benefit for developers and organizations utilizing Claude Code, potentially reducing their daily token expenditures by up to 90% through optimized usage.

### [Survival of the Fitted: Qwen3.6-27B’s Jacobian Lens Reads and Steers Qwen3.8-27B](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vpa5cv/survival_of_the_fitted_qwen3627bs_jacobian_lens/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Research investigated the transferability of an interpretability lens, specifically applying the Jacobian lens fitted for Qwen3.6-27B to the newer Qwen3.8-27B model without refitting. Despite Qwen3.8-27B shipping 113 days later with an undocumented training relationship, the transferred lens effectively read latent entities in 40 two-hop prompts, maintaining median ranks near the top of the 248,320-token vocabulary \(e.g., rank 17 at layer 48 vs. 4 on the home model\). Furthermore, steering directions derived from the Qwen3.6 lens successfully removed specific concepts like &quot;paradox&quot; from Qwen3.8&\#x27;s output while preserving coherence. This demonstrates that cross-checkpoint transfer is measurable for this specific lens family, model line, and version step with matched architecture and tokenizer, suggesting that interpretability tools may not always require refitting with every model update.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/imstilllearningthis · Aug 15, 18:24

**「Background」** Qwen models are a series of large language models \(LLMs\) developed by Alibaba Cloud, including open-weight versions like Qwen3.6-27B and Qwen3.8-27B, trained on extensive datasets across numerous languages. The Jacobian lens \(J-lens\) is an interpretability tool, developed by Anthropic, designed to observe and understand the internal processing signals within LLMs, often referred to as &quot;J-Space,&quot; before they generate output.

**「Impact」** This research indicates that interpretability lenses, such as the Jacobian lens, can retain significant effectiveness across minor version updates of large language models like Qwen without requiring a full refit, potentially streamlining the maintenance and application of AI interpretability and steering tools.
